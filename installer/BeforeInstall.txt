SEED Destiny Online 0.3.1 - experimental Windows release

Installs the private-room launcher and modified Play! emulator for NM00034 SEED Destiny: Rengou vs. Z.A.F.T. II.

Each player supplies their original DVD ISO and dongle BIN. No separate BIOS is needed. The installer does not include or download original game files.

Local linked 2-on-2 gameplay has been verified. Use Original Vulkan graphics. Enhanced OpenGL currently has reported flickering and missing scenery on an RTX 5090; its visual stability is not verified. Internet matches between separate PCs still need qualification. Hosting requires a reachable address, router forwarding and firewall access; installation does not modify those settings or your VPN.

Installation is for your Windows account. Uninstalling preserves separate settings and saves. Open-source licenses and corresponding sources are included.
