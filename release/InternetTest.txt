SEPARATE-PC INTERNET MATCH CHECK

Use four Windows PCs, with at least one on a different Internet connection.
Each player needs their own original disc ISO and dongle BIN. Use 0.4.0 on
the host and clients to share game-link status and team preferences.

1. Host TCP 7443 on a reachable address. Share the invitation and passcode
privately. Each guest uses Check connection; TLS must verify successfully.
2. Enter all four players, choose preferred teams, and Join & launch. The host
uses Launch my game. Record the reported server RTT/variation and timeouts.
3. Wait for all original games to link. Insert credit, start, and choose the
same actual teams inside the game. Confirm every viewpoint responds to input.
4. Play a complete timed round through results on every PC. Record slowdowns,
desynchronization, freezes, errors and whether all players reach the next menu.
5. In a separate test match, leave/disconnect one PC and record recovery. Do
not assume the emulator supports seamless in-game rejoining.

Connection checks and local four-client tests cannot substitute for this.
Do not share private-key.pem, passcodes, ROM data or saved machine states in
public reports. Keep the VPN configuration intact while diagnosing routes.
