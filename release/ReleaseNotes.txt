SEED Destiny Online 0.4.0 - solo, controllers and private-room improvements

Play solo starts the original arcade game without a room or Internet server.
Its fresh cabinet setup and saves are separate from online seats.

The Controls tab adds Xbox and keyboard presets, a live XInput tester, safe
backups and instructions for native F1 remapping. Custom mappings are preserved
until the user explicitly selects another preset. Physical controller input
remains unverified; no XInput controller was connected during qualification.

Join & launch combines authenticated room entry and game startup. The newer
host shares preparation/link/error status and preferred teams. Teams remain
selected inside the original game. Connection checks verify TLS identity,
while joined clients measure server round trips, variation and timeouts.
These are connection measurements, not proof of Internet game performance.

The stable separate Enhanced window from 0.3.3 is retained. The black scenery
defect was traced to a reused framebuffer retaining the wrong pixel format.
The fix restored mountains and sky in both frame buffers. A cold 3x gameplay
run captured 40 frames without the previous large black regions. This was
verified on the RTX 5090; other GPUs and locked-60-fps performance need testing.

Validation includes real local socket/TLS tests, controller preset preservation,
GUI workflows, and a fresh solo launch through the actual launcher. The earlier
0.3.0 four-cabinet timed-round test is historical and has not been repeated with
0.4.0. Full separate-PC Internet matches, other GPUs and clean-machine setup
remain outstanding. Original game data is excluded from distribution.
